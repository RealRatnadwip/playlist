# playlist.ratnadwip.com

A personal music archive, listening journal, and playlist collection belonging to Ratnadwip Sarkar.

---

### What this is

Think of it as a glorified Spotify profile, but with a much more personal purpose.

Not a startup. Not a SaaS dashboard. Not another generic streaming clone.

A place where:
- Visitors can see what I’m currently listening to in real time.
- Playlists are kept not for algorithms, but for reasons.
- Each playlist has a story — what was happening in my life, why these songs were gathered, who they were for, and the quiet memories attached to them.
- Songs can be opened directly in Spotify to listen to, without fake playback controls or algorithmic noise.

Music has always been an emotional anchor. This website is simply the shelf where those records are kept.
